2. What the app is

VictoryForm is a local Android training and nutrition app. You can log workouts, manual body weight, and food, and create encrypted backups. Most data stays on your device. The app does not require an account.

3. What is active now

In the regular app version described here, the following is active:

  • Local workout logging (sessions, exercises, sets, manual activities).
  • Manual body weight with local history and chart.
  • Food diary, custom foods/recipes, and nutrients stored locally.
  • Search/scan via Open Food Facts (see section 8).
  • Encrypted local backup (.avena) that you export and restore yourself.
  • In-app wipe / deletion of local data.
  • Optional Health Connect connection for reading workout sessions (READ_EXERCISE) and manual import of one selected session at a time (see section 5).
  • Optional steps reading via Health Connect (READ_STEPS) to show today’s step aggregate on Progress after your consent (see section 5). Totals are not stored permanently in this version.
  • Optional weight reading via Health Connect (READ_WEIGHT) to show recent weight samples temporarily on Progress after your consent (see section 5). HC weight is not saved automatically to local weight history in this version.

Automatic workout import is not enabled in this version.

5. Health Connect

Connecting is optional and does not require a VictoryForm account.

5.1 Status in the regular app now

  • After you connect Health Connect, VictoryForm may request read access to workout sessions (READ_EXERCISE).
  • “Browse available sessions” temporarily shows which sessions exist. Nothing is saved merely because you browse the list.
  • You choose which session to import. The app imports one session per confirmed action — not several at once.
  • Import happens only after your active confirmation.
  • When a session is imported, the following is stored locally in workout history: activity type (or your classification), start time, duration, optional distance, that the source is a Health Connect import, an external identifier used for deduplication, and optionally the source app package name (e.g. the fitness app the session came from). The app does not invent sets/reps and does not request heart rate or a GPS route.
  • The same session is not imported again as a duplicate (dedup via external identifier).
  • After you explicitly connect steps, VictoryForm may request read access to steps (READ_STEPS). The app reads today’s aggregate via Health Connect (COUNT_TOTAL / equivalent platform aggregate) to avoid double-counting across sources. Steps are shown on Progress and held in temporary memory; they are not stored permanently in local history, backup, or cloud in this version.
  • Automatic steps refresh on app open/resume runs only after you have connected steps; missing permission stops reading without opening a new dialog.
  • After you explicitly connect weight, VictoryForm may request read access to body weight (READ_WEIGHT). The app reads recent samples (up to about 30 days) via Health Connect and shows them temporarily on Progress. HC weight is kept separate from manual weight history and is not saved automatically locally, in backup, or in the cloud in this version.
  • Automatic HC weight refresh on app open/resume runs only after you have connected weight; missing permission or an empty period stops reading without opening a new dialog and does not overwrite manual entries.
  • When steps and weight are enabled, the production manifest declares READ_EXERCISE, READ_STEPS, and READ_WEIGHT — no other HC permissions.
  • The app does not request write access to Health Connect and does not request ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION.

5.2 Not enabled yet

  • Automatic saving of HC weight into local weight history (persist/import) — not active; manual weight logging works as before.
  • Automatic workout import (without your selection/confirmation) — not active.

You can disconnect in the app and/or revoke permissions in system Health Connect settings. New reading then stops. Local data already saved is not automatically deleted on disconnect.

6. Local storage, backup, deletion

  • Ordinary app data is stored locally on the device.
  • You can export an encrypted backup and restore it.
  • You can wipe local data using the in-app delete function.
  • Backup files you store outside the app are under your control.

7. Account, cloud, and paid services

VictoryForm does not require an account. You can use workouts, food, weight, and local .avena backup without signing up.

When a voluntary account is enabled (first in a separate QA test build, later possibly in the regular app):

  • Sign-in uses email and a one-time code (OTP), not a password stored in the app.
  • Your email is processed by Supabase Auth in the EU Frankfurt region to send and verify the code and maintain a session.
  • Sign-in and sign-out do not delete local workout, food, weight, or profile data.
  • Sign-in does not automatically upload guest data to the cloud. Sync starts only after a separate, explicit “bring local data” step (when that feature is enabled).
  • Encrypted local .avena backup remains independent of the account.

Premium subscriptions and paid network services are not active in this policy. Cloud mirroring of training data is not enabled in the regular app version described here.

8. Transfers to servers and third parties

Training, weight, and food-diary data are not sent to VictoryForm’s own servers in the active features described here.

Open Food Facts (when you search or scan):

  • Barcode lookup: the barcode is sent to world.openfoodfacts.org (product API) with requested fields and a descriptive User-Agent.
  • Text search: your query is sent to search.openfoodfacts.org with parameters such as page, page size, language preferences, and requested product fields.
  • A request may therefore include what you typed/scanned, technical HTTP headers (including User-Agent), and ordinary network metadata (e.g. IP address at the recipient).
  • The app does not use an API key for Open Food Facts. See also Open Food Facts’ terms and privacy.

When you use Health Connect for workout sessions, data is read via the on-device platform API. VictoryForm does not upload Health Connect data to its own cloud services in the described flow.

No advertising or analytics SDK is part of the reviewed app core for this policy.

10. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability (where applicable), and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet). Contact victoryform.app@gmail.com. You can also wipe local data in the app and revoke Health Connect permissions in the system.

11. Retention

Data remains on device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or soft-delete rules apply. Backup files outside the app are under your control.
